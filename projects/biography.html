About Me

The Person
Behind the Code

I'm ZEnum, a full-stack web developer currently based in Amsterdam, Netherlands. I was born and raised in Nigeria, where I fell in love with problem-solving through software while still a teenager.

Over the last five years, I've progressed from delivering freelance landing pages to architecting multi-tenant SaaS platforms used by tens of thousands of active users. I thrive at the intersection of elegant design and performant engineering.

My philosophy is simple: the web should be fast, beautiful, and work for everyone. I obsess over Core Web Vitals, accessibility standards, and the tiny polish details that transform a good product into a great one — the kind users talk about.

When I'm not writing code, you'll find me contributing to open source, mentoring junior developers online, or exploring Amsterdam's cycling routes with a good podcast on.
